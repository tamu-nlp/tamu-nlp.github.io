Texas A&M University College Station, TX

Natural Language Processing Group

We study natural language processing in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering. Our research spans event-centric NLP, discourse and dialogue, the robustness and safety of large language and vision-language models, multimodal and multilingual NLP, and LLM agents for scientific discovery.

Members

Faculty

Ruihong Huang

Associate Professor

Research and background

Event-centric NLP, discourse analysis, narrative understanding, dialogue and pragmatics, and the safety and morality of LLMs.

Ruihong has been at Texas A&M since 2015, following a PhD at the University of Utah and a postdoc in Stanford's NLP group, and is a recipient of an NSF CAREER award.

Research page Google Scholar

Kuan-Hao Huang

Assistant Professor

Research and background

Robustness and safety of LLMs and VLMs, multimodal reasoning and alignment, and multilingual understanding.

Kuan-Hao joined Texas A&M in 2024 after a postdoc at UIUC and a PhD at UCLA.

Research page Google Scholar

Yu Zhang

Assistant Professor

Research and background

Data mining and NLP for scientific discovery, including LLM agents for research, LLM agents with structural knowledge, and applications in science.

Yu completed a PhD at UIUC under Jiawei Han, with a dissertation that received the ACM SIGKDD Dissertation Award Runner-Up.
